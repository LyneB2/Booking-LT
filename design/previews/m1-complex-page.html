Playa Serena Residences

Beachfront condominiums around a quiet garden pool, a short walk from town.

1 / 5

About the complex

Playa Serena has 42 privately owned condos in three low buildings set back from the sand. Each condo is rented by its own host; the complex team looks after the grounds, the pool and the front desk.

The beach is calm in the morning and busier after lunch. Town, the fish market and two supermarkets are within a ten-minute walk.

Amenities

  • Outdoor pool, 8 am – 8 pm
  • Direct beach access
  • Gated parking, 1 space per condo
  • Front desk, 7 am – 11 pm
  • Wi-Fi in common areas
  • Backup generator

Complex rules

  1. Quiet hours are 10 pm to 8 am.
  2. All guests are registered with the front desk before arrival.
  3. No glass in the pool area.
  4. Pets are allowed only where the condo's host permits them.

Each condo can add its own house rules on its listing.

Location

Calle de la Playa, Samaná Peninsula. Exact directions are shared with confirmed guests.

Gallery with fewer photos

Desktop layouts chosen by photo count. No empty tiles are ever shown. On phones every count uses the same swipeable gallery with a counter.

1 photo: one wide image. No "Show all photos" button.

2 photos: two equal halves.

3 or 4 photos: one large image and two stacked. A fourth photo is reached through "Show all photos".

5 or more photos: one large image and four smaller ones. The rest are in "Show all photos".

Long complex names

Residencial Playa Serena Beach & Garden Condominiums

Names over 30 characters use the smaller heading size (36 px desktop, 28 px phone) and wrap onto balanced lines. Names are never cut off.
